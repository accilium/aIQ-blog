Welcome to **aIQ**, a blog about artificial intelligence — the ideas behind it,
the systems we build with it, and the practical lessons we pick up along the way.

## What you'll find here

- **Deep dives** into models, papers, and techniques worth understanding.
- **Engineering notes** from building real AI products: evals, prompts, infra.
- **Short takes** on what's changing in the field week to week.

## Why another AI blog?

Most writing on AI is either too hand-wavy or too in the weeds. We aim for the
middle: concrete enough to be useful, broad enough to be readable.
