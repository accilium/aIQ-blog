Welcome to **aIQ**, a blog about accilium's artificial intelligence journey — the ideas behind it,
the systems we build, and the practical lessons we pick up along the way.

## What you'll find here

- **Deep dives** into models, papers, and techniques worth understanding.
- **Engineering notes** from building real AI products: evals, prompts, infra.
- **Short takes** on what's changing at accilium as we ship new builds of accilium week to week.

## Why another AI blog?

We are tired of all the writing on LinkedIN and want to share insights on our journey in a way that feels right for us.
GitHub and Github pages feels right, as most of the thinking we produce is written in markdown and managed in Git. We are also committed to open-source skills and assets we create in public respostories to inspire others to follow us. 

Stay tuned — more posts coming soon.
